About & Contact

RoamLo is a travel budget and currency planning tool designed to help travelers understand how much a trip may actually cost before they book. It brings together estimated daily expenses, local currency information, and trip planning details in one place so budgeting for international travel is easier to understand.

Planning a trip abroad usually means doing currency math in your head, guessing at daily costs, and hoping your budget holds up once you land. RoamLo imagines a single page that converts costs into dollars up front and lays out a realistic daily budget, so there aren't any surprises once you're actually there.

Future versions of RoamLo will include interactive budget inputs, live currency conversion, destination weather, and more personalized trip estimates. The goal is to eventually let travelers enter their destination, trip length, and spending preferences to create a budget that fits their travel plans.
