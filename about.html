About RoamLo

RoamLo is designed to make international travel budgeting easier. Instead of switching between currency converters, travel websites, weather apps, and calculators, RoamLo aims to bring useful trip planning information together in one simple experience.

Travelers will be able to choose a destination, view estimated daily expenses, convert local prices into their home currency, and build a budget based on the length of their trip. Future features may also include destination weather and different spending levels for budget, standard, or higher-end travel.

Project: RoamLo
Focus: Travel budgeting and currency planning
Development: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and travel APIs

Planned Features

RoamLo will begin as a simple travel budgeting website and grow into a more interactive planning tool. Planned features include custom trip budgets, live currency conversion, destination weather, trip-length calculations, and personalized spending estimates. The long-term goal is to help travelers quickly answer one important question: "How much should I realistically budget for this trip?"
